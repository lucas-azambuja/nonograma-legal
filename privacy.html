Privacy Policy — Nonogram Picross

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Nonogram Picross app ("the app"), developed by Lucas Azambuja.

Questions: lucas.azambuja@foregon.com.

1. Summary

  • The app works offline and has no account, login or sign-up. We never ask for, store or transmit your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Solved puzzles, times and settings never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.
  • What does leave the device is advertising data, collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve and measure ads. It is the only data transmission that happens.
  • If you buy the ad removal, the ads SDK is no longer initialised and that transmission stops.

2. Data we collect ourselves

None. We run no server, we keep no user database, and we receive no information about you.

3. Data collected by the Google ads SDK

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google operates that SDK and acts as an independent controller of the data below, and shares it with its advertising partners.

DataPurposeCan you turn it off?
Android advertising ID and App Set ID Serving ads, measuring performance, fraud prevention Yes. Android → Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
IP address, also used to estimate approximate location (city/region) Serving ads in the right market and language, fraud prevention No — it is inherent to the connection. The app requests no location permission and never accesses GPS
In-app interactions (app launch, ad taps, video views) Measuring ad performance Not separately
Diagnostic information (device model, OS version, SDK errors) Making ads work and diagnosing failures Not separately

This data is transmitted encrypted. Google's processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Ads: what the app does and never does

This app publishes its own ad ceiling, in writing:

  • At most 1 full-screen ad for every 2 completed puzzles.
  • Never less than 90 seconds between two ads.
  • No ads at all in the first 3 puzzles of a new installation.
  • No ad inside the puzzle. Ever.
  • Video only if you ask for one — always with a prompt first, and you can say "Not now".

A full-screen ad you didn't ask for can appear at one single moment: when you return from a completed puzzle to the selection screen. There is no app-open ad and no banner on any screen.

Videos are rewarded ads and only happen if you tap to request one, with the reward stated first — they are the only exception to the lines above, and they exist because they are your choice. Nothing in the game is unlocked by an ad: there are no lives, no energy, no currency.

Buying the one-time ad removal stops all full-screen ads permanently and the ads SDK is no longer loaded.

5. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

This version of the app is not available in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. When it becomes available there, it will show you a consent form operated by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before the first ad, where you choose between personalised and non-personalised ads — and you will be able to change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Until then, no consent form is shown, because the app is not distributed in those regions.

6. Purchases

The ad-removal purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive, see or store payment data — no card, no name, no address. We receive only the confirmation that the purchase exists, and store that confirmation locally on your device.

7. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and is not distributed through Google Play Families. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child used the app and data was processed, write to lucas.azambuja@foregon.com.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no database about you, there is nothing on our side to correct, export or delete. In practice:

RightHow
Delete local dataUninstall the app, or Android → Settings → Apps → Nonogram Picross → Storage → Clear data
Stop advertising collectionAndroid → Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID; or buy the ad removal
Request information, correction or objectWrite to lucas.azambuja@foregon.com. We reply within 15 days
Data held by GoogleThrough Google's own controls: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

Controller for data processed by this app: Lucas Azambuja — lucas.azambuja@foregon.com.

9. Retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Local data stays on the device until you delete it. Retention of advertising data is determined by Google.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it and the new version lives at this same URL.
